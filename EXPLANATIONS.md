# Explanations

Add one entry for each slug in `completed.txt`.
The instructor grades the explanations by review.
CI prints a warning when an entry is missing, but the points stay.

Write each entry as a heading with its slug and three short paragraphs:
what the program must do, how your solution does it, and why it works.

## <slug>

One sentence on what the program must do.

How your solution does it. Name the instructions, registers, or data that do
the work.

Why that is enough, and one case you checked.
